# Code of Conduct

EchoSub is a project about understanding each other across languages. We want its community to reflect that: welcoming to everyone, whatever their language, background, experience level, identity or opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors write in their second or third language.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that decisions are sometimes made differently than you would make them.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, sexualized content, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, the Discord server — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, Mohammad Al-Safadi, at [mPhpMaster@gmail.com](mailto:mPhpMaster@gmail.com). All reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community.
